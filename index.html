🧠 ABOUT THE PROJECT

MadFly Lab is an open 3D simulation sandbox built on the real biological connectome of Drosophila melanogaster. Rather than driving an agent with engineered heuristics or artificial neural net approximations, MadFly Lab directly simulates the anatomical wiring, synaptic weights, and biophysical sensory loops mapped from HHMI Janelia's MaleCNS v1.0 dataset.

The project connects high-resolution WebGL 3D physical environments with neuromorphic rate-model dynamics. Visual threats, olfactory plumes, and mechanosensory pokes propagate through real descending motor pathways, allowing researchers and developers to observe biological intelligence in action.

🛠️ HOW IT'S BUILT

👁️ Bi-Splayed Eye & Looming Pipeline

Two 40° splayed eye cameras render compound retinas with LC4/LPLC2 motion-opponency looming detectors, directly driving the real Giant Fiber (DNp01) escape takeoff circuit.

👃 Olfactory Chemotaxis

Spatial 3D odor gradients stimulate real Odorant Receptor Neurons (ORN glomeruli DM1, VA6, CO2), steering locomotion via descending pathways (DNa01) and dopamine payout channels (PAM11).

⚡ Connectome Neural Runtimes

Simulates how neural activity propagates through sparse connectome networks on CPU/GPU (Mode A), or through optimized behavioral subgraphs directly in the browser (Mode B).

🧪 Interactive Stations & Optogenetics

Features 9 interactive arena stations (hazard fans, toad tongue strikes, light switches), a 3D soma point-cloud optogenetics laser, and tethered reinforcement learning rigs.
